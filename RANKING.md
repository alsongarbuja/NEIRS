### 📊 Institute Ranking Criteria: Detailed Metrics

#### Academic Ranking (Performance & Quality)

This component evaluates the core educational excellence and outcomes of the institution.
Metric | Professional Terminology | Description
--|--|--
Graduation Rate | Completion and Retention Rates | "Measures the percentage of students who successfully graduate within a standard timeframe (e.g., 4 or 6 years), often paired with the rate of students who return for their subsequent year (retention)."
Curriculum & Program Quality | Program Accreditation and Rigor | "Assesses the quality, relevance, and industry alignment of academic programs, often verified by third-party accreditation bodies."
Student to Faculty Ratio | Instructional Staff Ratio | "A key measure of individualized attention, representing the number of students per full-time faculty member."
Professor/Teacher Ratings | Faculty Effectiveness and Evaluation | "Incorporates structured, unbiased ratings of instructional staff effectiveness, teaching quality, and mentorship, as derived from the Professor Rating feature."
Research Output | Research Output and Faculty Credentials | "Measures faculty involvement in research (publications, grants) and the percentage of faculty holding terminal degrees (Ph.D., etc.)."

#### Infrastructure Ranking (Facilities & Resources)

This component assesses the physical and technological environment supporting the student body and academic activities.
Metric | Professional Terminology | Description
--|--|--
Labs | Specialized Learning Facilities | "Evaluation of research and instructional laboratories, workshops, and specialized technology centers based on capacity, modernity, and accessibility."
Libraries | Information and Resource Centers | "Assessment of physical library spaces, digital resource access, e-journals, and the availability of professional library support staff."
Parking | Campus Accessibility and Logistics | "Evaluation of campus traffic flow, public transportation integration, and the availability/cost of student and faculty parking facilities."
Tech Infra | Technology Infrastructure | "Measures the quality and reliability of Wi-Fi, campus network speeds, and availability of up-to-date computing resources and learning management systems."

#### Other Ranking (Support & Opportunity)

This component focuses on the broader ecosystem of student support, institutional reach, and post-graduation opportunities.
Metric | Professional Terminology | Description
--|--|--
Capacity | Institutional Enrollment and Accessibility | "The total number of students the institute can accommodate, often considered alongside applicant acceptance rates."
Career Support | Post-Graduation Outcomes and Services | "Measures the effectiveness of career counseling, job placement rates, alumni networking, and availability of internships and industry connections."
Student Life | Student Services and Campus Diversity | "Comprehensive evaluation of non-academic support (counseling, health services), extracurricular activities, and commitment to student body diversity."
Specialized Facilities | Auxiliary and Recreational Facilities | "Evaluation of facilities outside of the classroom environment, such as student centers, athletic complexes, performance venues, and dining services."
