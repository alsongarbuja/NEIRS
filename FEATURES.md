### 🚀 Key Features of NEIRS.

1. Ranking Institutions

> This will be the most critical and initially prioritized feature of the project. Our goal is to develop a comprehensive, fair, and unbiased ranking system for educational institutions based on a diverse set of criteria. This foundational feature will drive the core value proposition of NEIRS.

The overall Institutional Ranking will be divided into three major, weighted components to provide a holistic score:

- [**Academic Ranking (Performance & Quality)**](https://github.com/alsongarbuja/NEIRS/blob/main/RANKING.md#academic-ranking-performance--quality): This component consists of criteria directly related to the academic performance and educational quality of the institute.
- [**Infrastructure Ranking (Facilities & Resources)**](https://github.com/alsongarbuja/NEIRS/blob/main/RANKING.md#infrastructure-ranking-facilities--resources): This component is made up of criteria related to the physical and technological infrastructure that supports learning.
- [**Other Ranking (Support & Opportunity)**](https://github.com/alsongarbuja/NEIRS/blob/main/RANKING.md#other-ranking-support--opportunity): This component groups essential criteria that fall outside of strictly academic performance and core physical infrastructure, focusing on student support, opportunities, and overall capacity.

2. Rating Professors

> An important supporting feature, the professor/teacher rating system will ultimately serve as one of the key criteria in the overall institution ranking. This feature is designed to allow users to rate professors fairly on specific, predefined points, mitigating common biases.

3. Institute Comparision

> Included as a secondary feature for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), this will allow students and users to easily compare different institutes side-by-side using various filters. The aim is to simplify the decision-making process when choosing where to pursue their education.

4. Institute Infographic

> This secondary feature will provide a detailed infographic and profile for each institution, covering essential information such as location, official social media accounts, institutional capacity, and operational hours.

> [!NOTE]
> **Note on Data Integrity**: Gathering this information is crucial but presents a challenge. To ensure the data is unbiased and completely accurate, we ideally need the institute or its representative to provide it. However, we also intend to keep the entire NEIRS project as independent as possible from institutional influence to maintain our commitment to users. This balance is still being determined for the MVP.
